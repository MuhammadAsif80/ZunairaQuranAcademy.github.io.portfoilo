Our Courses

Comprehensive Quran & Islamic Education

✦

Each course is designed for a specific learning goal, taught one-on-one with expert guidance and proper Tajweed.

📗

Norani Qaida

نورانی قاعدہ

The foundation of Quranic reading. Learn Arabic letters, pronunciation, Harakat, and basic Tajweed rules step by step — perfect for children and adult beginners.

  • Arabic alphabet recognition
  • Makharij (letter articulation)
  • Harakat — Fatha, Kasra, Damma
  • Tanween, Madd, and basic rules
  • Ideal for children & beginners
📖

Nazra Quran

ناظرہ قرآن

Recite the Holy Quran fluently with correct Tajweed. Progress through the entire Quran with guided correction and proper pronunciation under expert supervision.

  • Fluent Quran recitation
  • Full Tajweed rules — Ikhfa, Idgham, Qalqala
  • Regular recitation tests
  • Complete Quran from Surah Fatiha to Nas
  • Suitable for all ages
🌟

Hifz ul Quran

حفظ القرآن

Become a Hafiz/Hafiza with our structured memorization program. Individual pacing, daily lessons, revision tracking, and consistent support from your personal teacher.

  • Structured daily memorization plan
  • Sabaq, Sabqi & Manzil revision system
  • Tajweed-certified memorization
  • Regular progress reports
  • For children & adults
☪️

Islamic Education

اسلامی تعلیم

Comprehensive Islamic studies covering daily prayers, Hadith, Islamic history, and Fiqh. Build a strong Islamic foundation for yourself or your children.

  • Namaz (Salah) — learning & method
  • Selected Hadith & Seerah
  • Basic Fiqh & Islamic values
  • Dua, Kalimas & Islamic manners
  • Age-appropriate curriculum
🤲

For New Muslims

نو مسلم

A specially designed, beginner-friendly program for those who have recently embraced Islam. Learn at a comfortable pace with extra care, understanding, and patience.

  • Arabic alphabet from scratch
  • Basic pillars of Islam
  • Salah — step-by-step guidance
  • Easy introduction to Quran
  • Private, judgment-free environment
👩‍🏫

For Sisters & Girls

خواتین کے لیے

All courses available exclusively with our qualified female teacher (Ustaza) — a comfortable, hijab-respecting, fully Islamic environment for women and girls worldwide.
